Collection from cookies

We may also collect personal information from you when you use or access our website or our social media pages. This may be done through use of web analytics tools, ‘cookies’ or other similar tracking technologies that allow us to track and analyse your website usage.   1. (Collection of financial information) We may collect financial information about you during the course of providing you our goods and services. We will only collect this financial information where you consent to such collection and directly provide us with this information.
  2. (Types of financial information) The financial information we collect may include the following:
    1. details about your assets, debts and liabilities, such as:
      1. the value of any property you own;
      2. details of the bank accounts you hold;
      3. details of any mortgages you have;
    2. your business and financial interests;
    3. any other financial information provided by you or a third party to us via our website or platforms, or otherwise provided by you or a third party to us.
  3. (Use of financial information) Your financial information will only be used for the purpose of:
    1. providing you with our goods and services, including our platforms;
    2. allowing lawyers to use our platforms to manage their clients and to improve and optimise their goods and service offerings and customer experience;
    3. assisting you with any queries or complaints relating to our goods and services;
    4. improving and optimising our goods and service offering and customer experience;
    5. complying with our legal obligations, resolving disputes or enforcing our agreements with you;
    6. sending you administrative messages, reminders, notices, updates, security alerts, and other information requested by you; or

    3. any other purpose which is permitted or required under applicable privacy laws.
